Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Namma Exam is operated by Clinisync Technology. This page explains what we collect, why we collect it, and what we do not do with it. By using Namma Exam you agree to this policy.

What we collect

When you register for a free account:

  • Your mobile number — this is how you log in
  • Your name — shown on your dashboard and your certificate
  • A password you choose
  • Your email address, only if you choose to give it. This is always optional.

When you take a practice exam we record your answers, score, time taken and attempt history, so you can see your result, reopen past attempts, track your progress and get a certificate.

If you use the planner, we store the notes and target you enter there.

How your password is stored

Your password is stored only as a cryptographic hash. We cannot read it, and neither can anyone who obtained a copy of our database. If you forget it, we cannot tell you what it was — we can only help you set a new one.

What we use it for

  • To create and run your account
  • To let you log in, resume an exam, and see your own history and progress
  • To generate your certificates
  • To contact you about your subscription, usually on WhatsApp using the number you registered with
  • To understand which papers are used most, so we know what to add next

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information to anyone
  • We do not share your data with advertisers
  • We do not post your information publicly. A certificate can be checked by its ID, and the mobile number shown there is partly hidden.
  • We never ask for your UPI PIN, card number or OTP

Payments

Subscription payments are made directly by UPI to our own account. We do not collect or store your card, bank or UPI credentials — that happens entirely inside your own UPI app. We only ask for a screenshot so we can confirm the payment and activate your subscription.

What is stored on your own phone

While you are taking an exam, your answers are saved on your own device as well as on our servers. This is so a weak or dropped connection cannot cost you your progress. That local copy is cleared when you submit.

Your choices

  • You can add or change your email at any time from your dashboard
  • You can change your password at any time
  • You can ask us to delete your account and everything in it — contact us and we will do it

Children

Namma Exam is meant for people preparing for government recruitment exams and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. Continuing to use Namma Exam after a change means you accept the updated version.
